# Powerlyze: Unlock your Strength Potential

Powerlyze is a fitness app designed to help powerlifters—and anyone interested in building strength—analyze and elevate their lifting game! With Powerlyze, you can log and assess your key lifts, compete against your friends, and see your progress on a global scale. Whether you're just starting out or pushing heavy weights, Powerlyze offers valuable insights to help you understand where you stand and where you can improve.


Key Features:

Analyze Your Strength: Input your squat, bench press, and deadlift weights, and Powerlyze will calculate your overall strength ranking. Compare your scores globally to see how you measure up!

Customized Insights: Discover your strengths and areas to improve. Powerlyze identifies which lifts you excel at and which need work, helping you create a more balanced and effective training program.

Leaderboards: Compete with friends and other users by climbing the global leaderboard, ranked by total lifting power.

Simple, User-Friendly Design: With an intuitive interface and easy-to-navigate options, Powerlyze makes it simple to stay on top of your strength goals.

Start powering up your fitness journey today with Powerlyze!

Deployed on App Store from 2024-2025. To run, download the project folder, open in XCode, and run the simulation with an iPhone of your choice.
